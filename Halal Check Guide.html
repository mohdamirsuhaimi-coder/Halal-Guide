How to check a halal certificate

Tap a step to open it.

Step 1: Find the logo and certificate

Look at the packaging or the shop entrance. A genuine certificate lists the company name, the premises or product covered, a certificate number and an expiry date.

Step 2: Search the official directory

Open the Malaysia halal portal and search the company or product name in the halal directory. If it appears with a valid status, it matches an official record.

Step 3: Compare the details

Does the name, address and validity date match what you saw? If something looks off, ask the staff or contact the certifying body.

Good to know

Signs like “Muslim-owned”, “no pork” or “Muslim-friendly” are not halal certificates. Only a recognised certifying body can issue one.
